WOOLLY WONDERS — THEME REFERENCE
=================================

COLORS
------
--cream:        #FAF6EF   (page background)
--warm-white:   #FDF9F4   (card/sidebar background)
--blush:        #F2D4C2   (soft accent)
--rose:         #D4856A   (mid accent)
--terracotta:   #B5603A   (primary brand color)
--sage:         #8FAF8A   (secondary green)
--sage-light:   #D6E8D3   (light green tint)
--sage-dark:    #4A6B46   (dark green)
--warm-brown:   #6B4C3B   (headings)
--dark:         #2C1F18   (dark text)
--mid:          #7A5C4E   (body text)
--light-text:   #A68E81   (muted text)

FONTS
-----
Headings:  Playfair Display (Google Fonts)
Body:      DM Sans (Google Fonts)

HOW TO CHANGE PRIMARY COLOR
-----------------------------
Find --terracotta: #B5603A and replace with your preferred hex.
Also update --rose: #D4856A for the lighter variant.

HOW TO ADD PRODUCTS
--------------------
Option A: Use Admin Panel → Add Product in the browser
Option B: Edit the DB.products array in index.html <script> section
